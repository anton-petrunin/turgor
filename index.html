The Tournament of Towns, State College, PA,

The Tournament of Towns — an international mathematical competition for school students.

  • Local organizers: Alexei Novikov and Anton Petrunin
  • Where:
    • Room 101, McAllister Building, Department of Mathematics.
  • When: 11am–4pm (you can leave earlier)
    • O-level (basic): October 4, 2026
    • A-level (advanced): October 18, 2026
  • Before you come: At least two days in advance, please send an email to aqp6@psu.edu that includes the following information:
    • Student's name.
    • Level (A, O, or both).
    • Student's grade.

The A-level is comparable in difficulty to the International Mathematical Olympiad; the O-level is simpler.
Only the three highest problem scores are taken into account.
